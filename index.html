02 / Current research

Can we trace an answer
back to its evidence?

Undergraduate thesis · Ongoing

Enhancing Trustworthy Retrieval-Augmented Generation through Fine-Grained Evidence Attribution

Two facts can each be supported while the relationship connecting them is not. Our research direction examines relation-aware, context-preserving attribution for claims requiring multiple evidence pieces.

Pre-Thesis I completed

Literature review and gap identification completed. Implementation and experiments are planned for later stages.

My contribution & research team +

I compiled and distributed the reading list, reviewed papers, synthesised candidate gaps across teammates’ findings and proposed a future direction through team discussion.

Supervisor: Dr. Amitabha Chakrabarty

Team: Alok Sarker Amit, Sumaiya Akhter Moon, Al Mueed Joarder Faiyaz and Md. Asadullah-Al-Galib.
